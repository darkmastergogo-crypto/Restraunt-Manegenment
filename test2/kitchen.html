    Order & waiter-call alerts

    Tap Enable sounds & notifications on each staff device and choose Allow. For locked-screen sound, allow notifications and sound for this website/browser in the phone settings, turn up notification volume, and check Do Not Disturb and battery/background restrictions.

    New orders use a boosted bright three-note chime and strong vibration where supported; waiter calls use a separate ding-dong. Alert sounds are on by default; device choices persist.     Waiter orders QR

    The waiter first installs the Waiter PWA. When its temporary session expires, opening the app launches its camera scanner. Scanning this pairing QR grants access only for the number of hours configured in Management. Replacing this QR immediately revokes active waiter sessions and old pairing QR codes.

    Reset dish sales only

    This clears ALL dates from the dish-sales report by excluding currently served orders. Original order records, bills, stock, numbering and active orders stay unchanged. Future served orders will count. This cannot be undone in this app. Export first.

    Reset all analytics

    This starts ALL dashboard analytics from zero, across Today, Month, Year, historical periods, charts and exports. Only orders placed after this reset will count. Earlier orders will not reappear even if marked served later.

    Order Records, bills, the separate Dish sales report, stock, menu, customer devices and live pending/kitchen work stay unchanged. This reset cannot be undone in this app. Export analytics first if needed.
