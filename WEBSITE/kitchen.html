    Voice & alarm sounds

    Custom voice pack

    Upload a JSON pack with embedded audio and phrase/digit segments. Download the working sample to see its format. Audio files stay on this browser, not shared with other devices. Voice choices save immediately on this browser; built-in alarm presets and Allow voice save for all kitchen staff.

    Enable order/call sound and Voice alerts on each device. While visible, unsilenced pending orders and requests repeat every 2 seconds; long announcements are sped up to finish before the next ring. Dismiss/mute stops repetition; Snooze pauses it. Waiter On the way also stops its alert. Requests take priority while their alert is open. Custom alarms use their first 1.8 seconds.

    Voice and custom tones work while the dashboard is open and visible, after enabling them on each device. Custom files and voice choices are browser-local; clearing site data removes them. Screen-off notifications use the phone’s system sound; a website cannot guarantee speech, custom tones or delivery when locked/closed.

    Prices & sizes

    Enable only the sizes you sell and set their prices. Turning this off restores single-price ordering. Existing orders keep their original size and price.     Permanent reset

    Request notification sound

    Enable Request alerts on each staff device. For locked-screen sound, allow notifications and sound for this website/browser in the phone settings, turn up notification volume, and check Do Not Disturb and battery/background restrictions.

    New orders use a bright three-note chime; waiter calls use a separate ding-dong. Enable each sound separately in Pending Orders and Requests. Both custom sounds work only while this page is open and visible. Background notifications use the phone's chosen system sound. A website cannot force maximum volume, bypass silent mode or guarantee delivery when the browser is closed or force-stopped.

    The test checks this device's notification display/sound, not the Supabase sender or closed-browser delivery. On iPhone, web push requires an installed Home Screen web app on supported iOS.

    Waiter orders QR

    Waiters scan this private QR to see active orders and customer calls without signing in. They can accept orders, mark them served and handle customer calls, but cannot open kitchen management or change menu items. Anyone with this link has those permissions, so share it only with staff.

    Reset dish sales only

    This clears ALL dates from the dish-sales report by excluding currently served orders. Original order records, bills, stock, numbering and active orders stay unchanged. Future served orders will count. This cannot be undone in this app. Export first.
